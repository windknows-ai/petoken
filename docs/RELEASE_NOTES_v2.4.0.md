# Petoken v2.4.0

Calendar reminders, stardust and her third form, Starsea.

## New

- **Meeting reminders from your calendar.** Paste a calendar's .ics link (Outlook, Google, iCloud and others offer one) in Settings › Calendar. A few minutes before a meeting (5, 10, 15 or 30) she holds up a card and your phone is told; while it runs she counts as Do Not Disturb; afterwards one notice says what the AIs finished, what waits for your approval and what failed meanwhile. Time zones, repeating meetings, moved and cancelled ones are understood. Nothing is read until a link is set.
- **Stardust.** Work that helped you adds stardust: a finished AI task (+3, +1 under a minute), AI work you accepted after review (+8), a focus round (+5) and every 10 minutes of AI work (+1, at most +40 a day). It is counted apart from companionship points. The Collection page shows how much you have and what comes next.
- **Star-ring skins.** 150 stardust unlocks the Meteor ring (the stars trail long tails), 400 the Aurora ring (with no tasks the ring flows in aurora colours). A new skin is put on when it unlocks; the Collection page switches between them.
- **Starsea Awakening.** 1000 stardust unlocks her third form: a starry long dress, a sheer starlit shawl, a small moon crown and nebula hair tips, with its own version of every pose and a short awakening when she changes. She only wears it when you choose it (right-click › Show › Starsea form, or the Collection page). Game mode and the late-night pajamas still come first. The Guide has a Starsea group.

## Fixed

- When Claude stops to wait for you without a permission prompt (a tool's own access dialog, a question or a plan to approve while permissions are bypassed), the pet and your phone are told too. Existing Claude instant-notification installs get the extra hook on the next start.

## Install

Download `Petoken-Setup-v2.4.0.exe`, check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and run it. Petoken 2.x updates itself when automatic updates are on.
