# Petoken v2.0.2

One fix and one new option.

- **The usage card can stay on.** The card above her head (the three rings)
  used to appear only while an AI was working. A new right-click option,
  **Usage card (also when idle)**, keeps it on when nothing runs. It is on by
  default; uncheck it to show the card only while an AI works. With no AI app
  open, the card shows each app whose limits are known.
- **Focus summary no longer cuts text off.** When a focus session ended, the
  summary card beside her could cut off long todo names. It now grows to fit.

v1.7.0 and later offer this update by themselves (Settings > General >
Updates). Otherwise download `Petoken-Setup-v2.0.2.exe` and verify it against
`SHA256SUMS.txt` from the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/windknows-ai/petoken/releases/tag/v2.0.2).
Everything in [v2.0.0](RELEASE_NOTES_v2.0.0.md) and
[v2.0.1](RELEASE_NOTES_v2.0.1.md) is unchanged.

# Petoken v2.0.2（中文）

一处修复，加一个新选项。

- **用量卡片可以一直显示。** 她头顶的用量卡片（三个圆环）以前只在 AI 工作时出现。右键菜单新增「用量卡片（待机也显示）」，勾选后没有任务时也一直显示。默认勾选；取消勾选，就只在 AI 工作时显示。没有打开任何 AI 应用时，卡片会显示已知额度的应用。
- **专注结束卡片不再截断文字。** 专注结束时，她旁边的总结卡片会把很长的待办名字截掉。现在卡片会自动变高，完整显示。

v1.7.0 及以后的版本会自己提示这次更新（设置 → 常规 → 更新）。也可以在 [GitHub 发布页](https://github.com/windknows-ai/petoken/releases/tag/v2.0.2) 下载 `Petoken-Setup-v2.0.2.exe`，并用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对。v2.0.0 和 v2.0.1 的其他内容不变。
