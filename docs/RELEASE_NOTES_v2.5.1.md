# Petoken v2.5.1

**English** · [中文](#中文)

## English

A smaller download: outfits now come when she owns them.

### Changed

- **The installer carries only Petoken itself.** Each outfit in the Stardust Wardrobe keeps just the pictures the mirror shows, so you can still try everything on. When you buy an outfit or wish for one, all of her actions in it download in the background (10–18 MB each), are checked against their SHA-256 and are kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CodexWisp\outfits`. She keeps her own look until the outfit is all here, then puts it on; the wardrobe shows how far the download is.
- Updating from 2.5.0 removes the outfit pictures it installed; the outfits you own download again by themselves.
- If a download fails, it is tried again when you change her look or start Petoken.

### Install

Download `Petoken-Setup-v2.5.1.exe`, check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and run it. Petoken 2.x updates itself when automatic updates are on.

## 中文

安装包更小了：服装改成拥有之后再下载。

### 改进

- **安装包里只有 Petoken 本体。** 星辉衣柜里每套服装只带镜子试穿要用的几张图，所以照样什么都能试穿。买到或许愿抽到一套服装后，她穿这套服装的全部动作会在后台下载（每套 10–18 MB），用 SHA-256 校验后存在 `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CodexWisp\outfits`。下载完成前她保持原来的样子，下载好就会穿上；衣柜里能看到下载进度。
- 从 2.5.0 升级时，会删掉 2.5.0 装进来的服装图片；你已经拥有的服装会自己重新下载。
- 下载没成功的话，换装或下次打开 Petoken 时会再试。

### 安装

下载 `Petoken-Setup-v2.5.1.exe`，用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对后运行。打开自动更新的 Petoken 2.x 会自己更新。
