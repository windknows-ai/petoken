# Petoken v2.2.0

Game mode with her second form, and notifications on your phone.

## Game mode
- **She transforms.** When a fullscreen game is in front (or you switch game
  mode on from her menu, under Show), she rises, the star ring flies behind
  her, armour forms on her piece by piece, a sword appears and a soft
  spotlight shows her off. About 13 seconds, or 6 at double speed. Leaving the
  game plays it in reverse.
- **She stays out of the way.** Task notices, reactions and pop-up cards stay
  quiet (still kept under Notifications; approval requests go back to Claude
  Code's terminal). The mouse goes to the game, except while it is over her or
  her info bar: then right-click, drag and long press work as usual. She never
  takes the focus from the game.
- **The ring behind her.** One star per running task, on a violet deep-space
  ring that breathes and turns slowly. Entering and leaving, the stars fly
  between her usual ring and this one.
- **A compact display.** Four small rings (5 hours and week for each app),
  or a bar with the limits, CPU, GPU, GPU temperature, VRAM or RAM, which you
  can drag anywhere. Or nothing.
- **Your settings.** Settings > Game mode: detection on or off, her size in
  game mode (separate from her usual size; she changes size smoothly and
  stays where she is), the ring style, the display, double-speed
  transformation, and a whitelist and blacklist of games (pick the .exe).
- **New poses.** Watching, tense, cheering you on, a water break, twirling her
  sword; the Guide shows all of them and the transformation.

## Notifications on your phone
- Settings > Phone sends finished, failed, waiting-for-you, low-limit and
  reminder notices to the free ntfy app (iOS and Android, no account) while
  you are away from the computer, focusing or gaming. Never during Do Not
  Disturb. The topic name is random and works like a password; project names
  can be left out.
- Notices now name the task the way Claude shows it, then the project.

## Also
- The usage card shows only the account limits (5 hours and the week) as two
  larger gauges; a nearly full context still notifies.
- Focus breaks start when you press OK, and the end of the break waits too.
- Her menu puts everyday actions first and every switch under Show; Settings
  show only the options that apply; every window fits its text in both
  languages.
- About the data explains why Claude's own stats count more tokens (they count
  every record line; Petoken counts each reply once).

v1.7.0 and later offer this update by themselves (Settings > General >
Updates). Otherwise download `Petoken-Setup-v2.2.0.exe` and verify it against
`SHA256SUMS.txt` from the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/windknows-ai/petoken/releases/tag/v2.2.0).

# Petoken v2.2.0（中文）

游戏模式（第二形态）和手机通知。

## 游戏模式
- **变身。** 全屏游戏在前台时（或者在她的菜单 → 显示 里手动打开），她会起身、星环飞到身后、铠甲一片片在身上成形、亮剑，最后在柔和的聚光灯下亮相。约 13 秒，两倍速约 6 秒。退出游戏时倒着播放。
- **不打扰你玩。** 任务提醒、她的反应和弹出卡片全部静音（照样记在「通知」里；审批请求交回 Claude Code 的终端）。鼠标在游戏上时穿过她，移到她或信息条上时右键、拖动、长按照常能用；她不会抢游戏的焦点。
- **身后的星环。** 每个进行中的任务一颗星，背景是缓缓旋转、会呼吸的幻紫色深空星环；进出游戏模式时，星星会在平常的星环和身后的星环之间飞过去、飞回来。
- **小巧的显示。** 四个竖排小圆环（两个 App 的 5 小时和一周），或者能显示额度、CPU、GPU、GPU 温度、显存、内存的信息条（可以拖到任何地方），也可以不显示。
- **都能自己调。** 设置 → 游戏模式：自动识别开关、游戏模式大小（和平常的大小分开，切换时慢慢变，位置不变）、星环样式、显示方式、变身两倍速，以及游戏白名单和黑名单（直接选 .exe）。
- **新动作。** 观战、紧张、给你打气、中场喝水、转剑；图鉴里能看到全部动作，还能直接看一遍变身。

## 手机通知
- 设置 →「手机」：离开电脑、专注或打游戏时，把任务做完、出错、等你确认、额度快用完和你的提醒推送到免费的 ntfy App（iOS 和 Android，不用注册）。免打扰期间不推送。频道名是随机的，相当于密码；可以选择不带项目名。
- 通知里先写 Claude 里显示的任务名，再写项目。

## 另外
- 用量卡片只显示账户额度（5 小时和一周），换成两个更大的圆环；上下文快满时照样会通知。
- 专注结束后点「好的」才开始休息，休息结束也会等你确认。
- 右键菜单把常用的放在最前，开关都收进「显示」；设置只显示和当前选择有关的选项；所有窗口在中英文下都能完整显示文字。
- 「关于数据」说明了为什么 Claude 自带的统计 token 更多（它每行记录都算，Petoken 每条回复只算一次）。

v1.7.0 及以后的版本会自己提示这次更新（设置 → 常规 → 更新）。也可以在 [GitHub 发布页](https://github.com/windknows-ai/petoken/releases/tag/v2.2.0) 下载 `Petoken-Setup-v2.2.0.exe`，并用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对。
