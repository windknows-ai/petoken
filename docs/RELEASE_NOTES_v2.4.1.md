# Petoken v2.4.1

Star-ring skins you can see, stardust explained, and smooth star trails.

## Changed

- **New ring skin effects.** Meteor: shooting stars streak through the night sky behind her, with twinkling heads and stardust tails, while small stars circle the ring. Aurora: aurora curtains rise from the ring behind her, flowing from green to violet, the ring glows in aurora colours and motes of light drift up. The ring, task stars and their trails keep their usual look; both skins move even when no AI is working, and a skin change shows at once.
- **Collection page**: the Stardust section lists how to earn it and shows a preview card for each skin and for the Starsea form, greyed with its price until unlocked; click one to wear it. The page scrolls, so nothing overlaps in a short window.

## Fixed

- When she is moved, the star ring glides after her; the star trails no longer draw straight streaks across the ring while it does.

## Install

Download `Petoken-Setup-v2.4.1.exe`, check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and run it. Petoken 2.x updates itself when automatic updates are on.
