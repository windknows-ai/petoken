# Petoken v2.4.1

New ring skin effects, an Aurora ring that moves with your music, stardust explained, and a smoother star ring.

## Changed

- **New ring skin effects.**
  - Meteor: shooting stars streak through the night sky behind her, with twinkling crystal heads and stardust tails; small stars twinkle and now and then a little shower comes; fine stars circle the ring.
  - Aurora: curtains rise from the ring behind her, folding and drifting in the stars' own colours, with motes of light; the ring glows in aurora colours.
  - Colours follow the Codex and Claude stars and the ring's lavender. The ring, task stars and their trails keep their usual look. Both skins move even when no AI is working.
- **The Aurora ring moves with your music.** While it is worn, the curtains rise and brighten with whatever your computer is playing and flash on the beat. Only the speakers' level is read (the number the Windows volume mixer shows); nothing is recorded.
- **Collection page**: the Stardust section lists how to earn it and shows a preview card for each skin and the Starsea form, greyed with its price until unlocked; click one to wear it. The page scrolls, so nothing overlaps in a short window.
- **The star ring follows her at once** when she is dragged or moved, instead of gliding in from where she was.

## Fixed

- No more straight streaks across the ring after she is moved.
- No flashing when dragging her or changing skins.
- The game-mode transformation: the spotlight fades in and out softly with no straight edges, and nothing flashes as it ends.

## Install

Download `Petoken-Setup-v2.4.1.exe`, check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and run it. Petoken 2.x updates itself when automatic updates are on.
