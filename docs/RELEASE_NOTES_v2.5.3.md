# Petoken v2.5.3

**English** · [中文](#中文)

## English

A usage card that moves with her, steady decorations in game mode, and a bigger daily sign-in.

### Changed

- **The usage card follows her.** It sits centred over her head, just above the highest thing she shows: her hair, or a crown, antlers or wings she wears. A new pose or headpiece eases it up or down; dragging her, it keeps up at once. With no room above her (near the top of the screen) it moves beside her.
- **Daily sign-in, a week-long round:** days 1, 3, 5 and 7 in a row give 500 stardust, days 2, 4 and 6 give 650; then the round starts again. Missing a day starts again from day 1. (This replaces +10 a day and +50 every seven days.)

### Fixed

- **Game mode:** what she wears no longer slips behind her or the ring. Game mode raises its windows twice a second and left her decorations out; they now keep their place right behind and in front of her.
- Head pieces no longer shake between two pictures of the same action (typing, above all): each action keeps one fit, and the piece moves only as far as her head does.

### Install

Download `Petoken-Setup-v2.5.3.exe`, check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and run it. Petoken 2.x updates itself when automatic updates are on.

## 中文

用量卡片跟着她走，游戏模式里的装饰不再乱动，每日签到奖励变大。

### 改进

- **用量卡片跟着她走。** 卡片对准她的头居中，放在她最高处的正上方：她的头发，或者戴着的王冠、鹿角、翅膀。换姿势、换头饰时卡片平滑地上下移动；拖动她时立刻跟上。她靠近屏幕顶上、上方放不下时，卡片挪到她旁边。
- **每日签到改成一周一轮：** 连续签到第 1、3、5、7 天各得 500 星辉，第 2、4、6 天各得 650，7 天后重新开始一轮；中间断一天就从第 1 天重新算。（取代原来的每天 +10、连续 7 天再 +50。）

### 修复

- **游戏模式：** 她戴的东西不会再跑到她或星环后面。游戏模式每秒两次重新排列窗口层级，以前漏掉了装饰层，现在装饰会固定在她身后和身前。
- 同一个动作的两帧之间（尤其是打字），头饰不再自己抖：每个动作固定一个戴法，头饰只跟着她的头移动。

### 安装

下载 `Petoken-Setup-v2.5.3.exe`，用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对后运行。打开自动更新的 Petoken 2.x 会自己更新。
