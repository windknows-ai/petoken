# Petoken v2.3.0

- **Late-night mode.** From 23:00 to 07:00 (choose your hours in Settings >
  Rest; optionally also during Do Not Disturb) she gets sleepy, changes into
  her moon pajamas and hugs her moon pillow. Every pose has a pajama version:
  she reads under a blanket while an AI works, waves good night for good
  news, sips warm milk and falls asleep on her pillow when you step away.
  Switch it for now under Show in her menu; the Guide has a Late night group.
- **Sitting reminder.** After an hour at the computer (30 to 90 minutes, or
  off) without a five-minute break she stretches and suggests getting up.
  Never while focusing or gaming; during Do Not Disturb only the stretch.
- **App time.** Reports > App time can record how long each program is in
  front, today, yesterday, this week or the last 7 days. Off until you turn
  it on; only program names and times are kept, on this computer.
- **More accurate AI working time.** Reports count tasks that run at the
  same time once and leave out long waits inside a turn (a background task,
  a used-up limit); work done by a subagent still counts. Claude task
  notices use the same time.

v1.7.0 and later offer this update by themselves (Settings > General >
Updates). Otherwise download `Petoken-Setup-v2.3.0.exe` and verify it against
`SHA256SUMS.txt` from the
[GitHub release](https://github.com/windknows-ai/petoken/releases/tag/v2.3.0).

# Petoken v2.3.0（中文）

- **深夜模式。** 晚上 11 点到早上 7 点（在设置 → 作息里自己定，开着免打扰时也可以），她会犯困，换上月夜睡衣，抱起月亮枕头。所有动作都有睡衣版：AI 干活时她盖着被子看书，有好消息就挥手说晚安，会捧着热牛奶，你走开一会儿就抱着枕头睡着。右键「显示」里可以临时开关，图鉴里多了「深夜」一组。
- **久坐提醒。** 连续用电脑一小时（可选 30 到 90 分钟，或者关闭），中间没离开过 5 分钟，她会伸个懒腰提醒你起来走走。专注和游戏模式时不提醒，免打扰时只伸懒腰不弹通知。
- **使用时长。** 报告 → 使用时长可以记录每个程序在前台用了多久，看今天、昨天、本周或最近 7 天。默认关闭；只记程序名和时间，只存在这台电脑上。
- **AI 工作时长更准。** 报告里几个任务同时跑只算一次时间，一轮里长时间的等待（等后台任务、额度用完）不算；交给子任务做的工作照样算。Claude 任务完成通知里的用时也按这个算。

v1.7.0 及以后的版本会自己提示这次更新（设置 → 常规 → 更新）。也可以在 [GitHub 发布页](https://github.com/windknows-ai/petoken/releases/tag/v2.3.0) 下载 `Petoken-Setup-v2.3.0.exe`，并用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对。
